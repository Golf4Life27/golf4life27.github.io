How the comparison works

Both columns use the same house, the same mortgage, property tax and HOA. What changes is everything else:

  1. Airbnb net = booked nights × nightly rate + turnovers × cleaning fee, minus the host fee, minus cleaning and supplies per stay, a co-host share and a repair reserve, minus fixed costs. Fixed costs include the utilities, internet, software, permits and higher insurance a furnished rental carries. An average month has 30.4 nights (365 ÷ 12).
  2. Long-term net = rent collected after vacancy, minus management and a repair reserve, minus fixed costs. On a lease the tenant usually pays most utilities.
  3. Occupancy to match = (long-term net + Airbnb fixed costs) ÷ (30.4 × what each booked night contributes after fees and per-stay costs). Below that occupancy, the 12-month lease earns more.
  4. Tie rent = the monthly rent at which the lease would earn exactly what the Airbnb does.
  5. Night cap: if your city limits short-term rentals to, say, 90 nights a year, booked nights can't go above 90 ÷ 12 a month no matter how high you set occupancy.

On paper the Airbnb wins, but by $47 a month, with only 1.1 occupancy points of cushion, and the $20,000 of furniture would take more than 35 years of that extra profit to earn back. Raising the lease rent by just $59 a month erases the difference. Cash-on-cash tells the same story: 1.2% for the Airbnb on $80,000 in, 0.7% for the lease on $60,000. Here, the lease is the calmer bet unless the Airbnb can reliably book well above 61%.

Now set the night cap to 60, as some cities have proposed: booked nights fall to 5 a month and the Airbnb loses about $1,794 a month. Check your city's short-term rental rules before you compare revenue.

What usually decides it

  • Rules first: permits, night caps, owner-occupancy requirements and HOA bans can end an Airbnb overnight. A lease is rarely affected.
  • Slow season: run the calculator with your market's off-season occupancy and rate, not the summer peak.
  • Your time: a self-managed Airbnb is a hospitality job. Put 20 to 25% in the co-host box to see what the comparison looks like if you pay someone to run it.
  • The fallback test: many investors only buy a short-term rental that still works as a long-term rental. If the lease side here is positive, the nightly income is upside rather than a bet.

FAQ

Is Airbnb more profitable than a long-term rental?

Gross revenue is usually higher, but net profit often isn't by much once you subtract the host fee, cleanings, supplies, furnished-rental utilities and insurance, and a reserve for wear and tear. Run the same property both ways: if the Airbnb only wins by a small amount or needs occupancy close to its current level just to tie, the lease is the safer choice.

What occupancy does an Airbnb need to beat a long-term rental?

It's the occupancy at which the Airbnb's monthly net equals the lease's monthly net. The calculator works it out as the long-term net plus the Airbnb's fixed costs, divided by 30.4 nights times what each booked night contributes after fees and per-stay costs. Compare it with what similar listings in your area actually book in the slow season.

Should I include furnishing costs in the comparison?

Yes. Furniture, linens, kitchenware and setup are cash you only spend for the Airbnb. The calculator adds them to the cash invested for the Airbnb's cash-on-cash return and shows how many months of extra profit it takes to pay them back.

How do city night caps change the math?

A cap limits how many nights you can book a year, so revenue stops growing above that point while the mortgage, taxes and insurance stay the same. Enter the cap in nights per year and the calculator limits booked nights to the cap divided by 12 each month.

Who pays utilities in each case?

With an Airbnb the host pays electricity, water, gas, internet and streaming. With a long-term lease the tenant usually pays most of them, though owners often keep water, trash or lawn care. Put whatever you'd still pay in the long-term utilities box.

What about taxes on rental income?

This calculator compares pre-tax cash flow. Short-term and long-term rentals can be taxed differently, for example depending on the average stay and how much you take part in running them, so check with a tax professional before deciding on tax grounds.
