A one-screen spreadsheet that turns price, loan and rent into monthly cash flow, cap rate, cash-on-cash return and the 1% rule, with vacancy, management, repairs and CapEx already built in.

"Rent minus mortgage" makes almost every deal look good. The costs that don't show up every month (vacancy, management, repairs and big-ticket replacements) are what turn a "$850 a month" rental into a $45 a month one.

This free calculator does the full math for you:

  • ✅ Mortgage payment (principal and interest) from price, down payment, rate and term
  • ✅ Net operating income after vacancy, management, repair and CapEx reserves, taxes and insurance

  • ✅ Cap rate, cash-on-cash return and the 1% rule
  • ✅ A plain-English quick verdict: negative, thin, or worth a deeper look
